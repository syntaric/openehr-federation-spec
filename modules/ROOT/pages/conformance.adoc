= 17. Conformance points (consolidated)

A numbered, testable list feeding xref:testing.adoc[§16]. Each maps to requirements and a test track.

[cols="1,5,2,1"]
|===
| CP | Statement | Requirements | Track

| [[cp-1]]*CP-1*
| The gateway exposes a conformant openEHR Query API and a basic patient query needs no federation-specific syntax.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n1[N1]
| 1

| [[cp-2]]*CP-2*
| The gateway accepts a `subject` predicate as façade input and *never* sends `subject` to a node.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n2[N2], xref:requirements.adoc#n5[N5]
| 1, 2

| [[cp-3]]*CP-3*
| `subject` is resolved to a per-node `ehr_id` via the cross-reference service (proposed: PIXm `$ihe-pix`), *outside AQL*.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n3[N3]
| 2

| [[cp-4]]*CP-4*
| Nodes are queried with *standard AQL keyed on `ehr_id`* only.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n7[N7]
| 2

| [[cp-5]]*CP-5*
| Undirected queries derive their node set (and consent) from localization; ask-all is an explicit fallback.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n4[N4], xref:requirements.adoc#n10[N10]
| 3, 4

| [[cp-6]]*CP-6*
| Directed `FROM ENDPOINT` / `ORGANISATION` selects the node set independently of patient resolution; `not-resolved` members are reported, not errored.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n11[N11]
| 3

| [[cp-7]]*CP-7*
| A `subject` column in the result is the *re-injected input*, never read from a CDR.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n5[N5]
| 2

| [[cp-8]]*CP-8*
| `DISTINCT` and `ORDER BY` are honoured at the Tier.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n13[N13]
| 5

| [[cp-9]]*CP-9*
| Default is pass-through (duplicates kept); opt-in `object_id` dedup keeps the originating copy for imported compositions.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n15[N15]
| 5

| [[cp-10]]*CP-10*
| Undirected aggregates are blocked unless cross-node-correct; a directed single-node aggregate is allowed.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n14[N14]
| 5

| [[cp-11]]*CP-11*
| Every in-scope node appears in `meta.endpoints[]` with a status; unresponsive nodes contribute no rows.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n16[N16]
| 4

| [[cp-12]]*CP-12*
| Partial results also surface as `OperationOutcome` warning/incomplete for FHIR consumers; status codes follow xref:partial-results.adoc#http-status-mapping[§11.2]; "found nowhere" → 200 + empty rows.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n6[N6], xref:requirements.adoc#n16[N16]
| 4

| [[cp-13]]*CP-13*
| The registry maps Organizations, Endpoints and every observed `system_id`/`creating_system_id` → CDR base URL.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n19[N19], xref:requirements.adoc#n20[N20], xref:requirements.adoc#n21[N21]
| 6

| [[cp-14]]*CP-14*
| Follow-up *reads* route to the owning CDR by `creating_system_id` (then `endpoint_id`, then ask-all); uids are never rewritten.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n22[N22]
| 6

| [[cp-15]]*CP-15*
| Versioned *writes* route to the single controlling CDR; unroutable writes are rejected; new objects target one chosen node.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n23[N23]
| 6

| [[cp-16]]*CP-16*
| Client identity is conveyed to the source CDR on every routed follow-up.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n24[N24]
| 7

| [[cp-17]]*CP-17*
| The client authenticates to the gateway; the gateway authenticates onward and propagates identity (signed JWT / Backend Services).
| xref:requirements.adoc#n25[N25]
| 7

| [[cp-18]]*CP-18*
| Access decisions needing gateway-invisible information are delegated to the source CDR.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n26[N26]
| 7

| [[cp-19]]*CP-19*
| Consent gates node inclusion at Step 1 and is re-checked at the node.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n27[N27]
| 7

| [[cp-20]]*CP-20*
| An openEHR Query API endpoint uses a defined `connectionType` (e.g. `openehr-rest-query`), not `hl7-fhir-rest` and not an informal string.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n19[N19]
| 6

| [[cp-21]]*CP-21*
| The gateway serves ITS-REST at its declared base URL with *no mandated prefix*; a client configured from the registry works without `/rest/openehr` or any other hard-coded prefix.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n28[N28]
| 9

| [[cp-22]]*CP-22*
| Both EHR addressing forms work: the canonical path `+{base}/v1/ehr/{ehr_id}+` and the AQL predicate `WHERE e/ehr_id/value = …` (and, where supported, `FROM EHR e[ehr_id/value=…]`).
| xref:requirements.adoc#n29[N29]
| 9

| [[cp-23]]*CP-23*
| `+OPTIONS {base}/+` returns the supported/unsupported ITS-REST areas, the accepted targeting mechanisms, dedup mode, timeout policy, *and the member endpoints behind the gateway* - without requiring a patient identifier.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n30[N30]
| 9

| [[cp-24]]*CP-24*
| A routed write returns `openEHR-federation-endpoint` (and SHOULD, `openEHR-federation-system-id`) with `Location`/`ETag` passed through unmodified.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n31[N31]
| 9

| [[cp-25]]*CP-25*
| The DEMOGRAPHIC API is not federated: it is either `501` or single-node-routed, and the behaviour matches what `OPTIONS` declares.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n32[N32]
| 9

| [[cp-26]]*CP-26*
| *No identifier leaks into the composed query.* With a directly identifying identifier supplied via `external_ref`, via `PARTY_IDENTIFIED`/`DV_IDENTIFIER`, in a projection and in the query string/headers, node-side capture shows the `ehr_id` and *no* identifier value in the dispatched AQL, path, query string or headers; a query that cannot be brought into that state is rejected `400`, not forwarded. Conversely, a write body is delivered unmodified.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n33[N33]
| 10

| [[cp-27]]*CP-27*
| A node is invocable on `ehr_id` alone, and its environment answers the patient-identifier → `ehr_id` cross-reference; no requirement is placed on what the node stores internally.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n34[N34]
| 10

| [[cp-28]]*CP-28*
| Endpoint targeting works *both* in AQL and via the `openEHR-federation-endpoint` request header, on the same stored query; conflicting node sets in one request are rejected `400`. A node set offered as a query parameter (`?endpoint=`) is *not* a supported targeting mechanism.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n35[N35]
| 3

| [[cp-29]]*CP-29*
| A write derived from a de-duplicated row reaches the *originating* CDR; a write whose only reachable holder is a copy is rejected `409` rather than forked, and suppressed endpoints stay visible in `meta`.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n36[N36]
| 5, 6

| [[cp-30]]*CP-30*
| Best-effort: with one node down, the query returns `200` with the reachable nodes' rows, `meta.complete = false`, and the failed node reported. Opt-in all-or-nothing, where offered, fails the whole query instead.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n37[N37]
| 4

| [[cp-31]]*CP-31*
| Timeouts are bounded and visible: a slow node is abandoned at the declared per-node timeout and marked `time-out` (never reported as empty), the overall budget is honoured, `latency_ms` is reported per endpoint, and a client `Prefer: wait=` can shorten but not extend the budget.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n38[N38], xref:requirements.adoc#n40[N40]
| 4

| [[cp-32]]*CP-32*
| Cross-node result shaping is correct: `ORDER BY` + `LIMIT n` returns the *global* top `n` deterministically; `OFFSET > 0` is rejected or computed correctly (never silently pushed down); an undirected aggregate is rejected `400` with a reason and never answered with per-node aggregate rows.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n39[N39], xref:requirements.adoc#n14[N14]
| 5

| [[cp-33]]*CP-33*
| _(Gateway.)_ `ehr_id` routing and integrity: a path `ehr_id` routes by explicit target / binding / index, a write is rejected `400` rather than ask-all-probed, and an `ehr_id` claimed by two nodes yields `409` plus an integrity incident - never a guess.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n41[N41], xref:requirements.adoc#n42[N42]
| 6, 11

| [[cp-33a]]*CP-33a*
| _(Federation operator / candidate node.)_ Admission conditions exist and are applied (xref:membership.adoc#identifier-conditions[§12b.2]): a candidate node demonstrates UUID-v4 (or equivalent) `ehr_id` generation, no `ehr_id` reuse across restores/migrations, no adoption of foreign `ehr_id` s on import, a `system_id` unique in the federation, and a working patient-identifier → `ehr_id` exchange. Verified at admission, not per request.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n42a[N42a]
| 11

| [[cp-34]]*CP-34*
| Definition handling: `+{base}/v1/definition/…+` routes to one explicitly chosen node and no merged catalogue is presented; where fan-out template upload is offered it is explicit, per-node reported, and a partial failure is reported as partial - never as success.
| xref:requirements.adoc#n43[N43]
| 9
|===
